HR Challenges & Solutions

Identified challenges from manager interview at Khana Khazana, Phagwara

🎙
Manager Interview Conducted
Date: 16 September 2026 · Location: Khana Khazana, Phagwara
HR Interview
01
🚪

Employee Retention

Employees leave after gaining experience for better opportunities, higher salaries, or different work schedules elsewhere.

Employees affected8
Root Causes:
  • Better job opportunities in other restaurants
  • Higher salary expectations over time
  • Demanding work hours during peak periods
  • Competition from urban restaurants
📊 Impact:
Severity
High
💡 Proposed Solution
  • Introduce performance-based incentives
  • Offer yearly appraisals / salary reviews
  • Create a growth track for loyal employees
  • Improve work schedule flexibility
02
🔍

Finding Skilled Employees

Recruiting experienced cooks, chefs, and skilled service staff is difficult due to limited local availability and competition.

Employees affected6
Root Causes:
  • Limited supply of experienced kitchen staff
  • High salary demands from experienced workers
  • Specialized culinary skills required
  • Competition from larger restaurant chains
📊 Impact:
Severity
Medium-High
💡 Proposed Solution
  • Post on Naukri.com, LinkedIn, local boards
  • Partner with local culinary institutes
  • Offer referral bonuses to existing staff
  • Build a talent pipeline proactively
03
📚

Training New Employees

Training during peak business hours is difficult; limited formal programs mean new hires take longer to become fully productive.

Employees affected10
Root Causes:
  • Restaurant busy during regular training hours
  • No dedicated trainer or HR professional
  • Training depends on experienced staff availability
  • Lack of structured training materials
📊 Impact:
Severity
Medium
💡 Proposed Solution
  • Implement this digital Training Tracker tool
  • Schedule training during off-peak hours
  • Create simple SOP booklets for each role
  • Assign a mentor to each new hire

📋 Interview Questionnaire & Responses

Q1What is the current number of employees at Khana Khazana?
There are currently 24 employees working across different departments including kitchen, service, delivery, support, management, and accounts.
Q2What are the different departments or work areas?
The restaurant operates through 6 departments: Kitchen (7), Service (6), Delivery (4), Cleaning/Support (3), Management (2), and Accounts/Admin (2).
Q3How do you hire new employees?
New employees are mainly recruited through employee references, local contacts, and direct applications. Online job portals are occasionally used for skilled positions.
Q4Can you tell me about the selection process?
Candidates go through a basic interview conducted by the manager. Selection is based on skills, experience, and suitability for the job role. References are verified for key positions.
Q5How do you give employees different jobs?
Employees are assigned based on their skills, experience, and the operational needs of the restaurant. During peak hours, some cross-department support occurs.
Q6Do you train new employees?
Yes. New staff receive practical on-the-job training guided by experienced employees or supervisors. The training covers food handling, customer service, and workplace procedures.
Q7How do you measure employee performance?
Performance is assessed through attendance records, work quality, customer feedback, and daily supervisor observations. Feedback is given informally when improvements are needed.
Q8What employee welfare facilities are offered?
Employees receive regular wages, scheduled breaks, leave as applicable, and meals during shifts. Management maintains open communication and tries to provide a supportive work environment.
Q9What are the main HR problems in your restaurant?
The main challenges are finding suitable experienced employees, retaining staff who seek better opportunities, and managing staffing during peak business periods.
Q10Do employees leave frequently?
There is some turnover. Employees may leave after gaining experience for better job opportunities, higher salaries, or due to work schedule requirements.
Q11Is it hard to find skilled employees?
Yes, particularly for kitchen and service roles. Finding candidates with the right skills and experience takes time, and salary expectations can sometimes be a barrier.
Q12How many more employees could possibly be needed in the future?
The management expects to need 2 additional employees — one in the Service department and one in Delivery — to meet increasing customer demand and maintain efficient operations.
